      Who We Are

      About Maze

      Maze is a Nairobi-based technology company specializing in home and commercial electronics installation products. Founded in 2016, we've grown to become one of Kenya's trusted suppliers of TV wall mounts, security guards, solar outdoor lighting, and extension cable solutions.

      We pair our products with professional installation services, making smart home upgrades accessible to homes, schools, hotels, and businesses across Kenya.
